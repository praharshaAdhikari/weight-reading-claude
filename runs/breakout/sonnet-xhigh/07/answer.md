## Design

**Strategy.** The ball moves one cell diagonally per step and the paddle moves one cell per step. The paddle catches the ball if it ends on the ball's landing column L (or on the column just before it). The network predicts L and walks the paddle toward it.

- **Linear features.** The ball, trail and paddle are one-hot, so the ball column bx, ball row by, trail column tx, trail row ty and paddle column p are all linear in the inputs.
- **Direction.** dx ≈ bx − tx and dy ≈ by − ty. On frames where the trail gives 0, a tie-break is used: bx ≤ 4 means moving right, and by ≤ 4 means moving down.
- **Landing prediction.**
  - Remaining steps are k = 9 − by when the ball is descending.
  - For an ascending ball I assume a brick bounce at row 4, so k = by + 2. This is a heuristic.
  - The unfolded column is u = bx + dx·k. The wall in this game holds the ball for one step, so the fold has period 20: L = u for u in 0..9, L = 19 − u for u in 10..19, and L = −1 − u for u < 0.
- **Move rule.** Move right if L > p, left if L < p, otherwise do nothing. In terms of u and p:
  - R ⇔ (u + p ≤ −2) or (u − p ≥ 1 and u + p ≤ 18)
  - L ⇔ (u + p ≥ 20) or (u + p ≥ 0 and u − p ≤ −1)

**Layer 1 (10 of 32 units used).**
- Unit 0 is the "moving right" flag and unit 1 is the "moving down" flag. Both are steep tanh units, so they are about ±1.
- Units 2–9 cover the four cases (right-down, right-up, left-down, left-up). Each case has two units:
  - a sum unit tanh(3·(u + p + offset)),
  - a difference unit tanh(3·(u − p)).
- Each of these is about −1, 0 or +1, where 0 means "exactly on target".

**Layer 2 (12 of 32 units used).** Each unit is an AND of the two case flags and one or two comparison flags. There are three units per case, covering the R and L conditions above. The conditions within a case never overlap.

**Output.**
- Left logit is 36 + 6·(sum of its 6 units), which gives about 12 when one fires and about 0 otherwise.
- Right is built the same way.
- No-op, up, down and fire have a constant logit of 3.5, so the default is to stay put.
