## Design

**Strategy.** The network predicts where the ball will be when it reaches row 8, the last row before the paddle. It moves the paddle into the "catch zone" there and otherwise does nothing.

- **Catch zone.** The code catches the ball when, after the paddle has moved, the paddle is at the ball's current column or at the column it is moving into. So the zone is {F(u), F(u+d)}, where u is the landing column in unfolded coordinates and d is the horizontal direction.
- **Wall reflections.** Side walls fold the unfolded line with period 20: F(m) = m for m < 10, and F(m) = 19 − m for m from 10 to 19.
- **Falling ball.** The prediction is exact: u = x + d·(8 − y).
- **Rising ball.** The network assumes the earliest possible bounce. That is row 4 (hitting a row-3 brick) for y ≥ 4, the next brick row inside the brick area, or the top wall from rows 0 and 1. A real bounce can only happen later, and the extra distance never exceeds the extra time, so the paddle can still adjust once the ball falls. Then u = x + d·k(y), with k = [9, 10, 7, 6, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9] for rows 0 to 8.
- **Just after a paddle hit.** If the ball came off the paddle's far side, its direction is opposite to its last displacement. For a ball rising at row 8, the target is therefore 9 − x, the one column that is in the correct zone for either kind of paddle hit.
- **Actions.** Move right if the paddle is left of the zone, left if it is right of the zone, no-op inside it. A 2-cell zone tolerates a one-step sticky overshoot. Logits are ±15, so the policy is effectively deterministic.

**Hidden layer 1 (10 units used):**
- **u0, V:** vertical direction, from the ball row and trail row, with a ±0.5 adjustment. If the ball has not changed row, it counts as falling in rows 0–4 (just bounced off a brick or the top) and rising in row 8 (just bounced off the paddle).
- **u1, D:** horizontal direction, an exactly linear ±1 score. Ball-column weights are [1,1,3,3,5,5,7,7,9,9] and trail-column weights are [0,−2,−2,−4,−4,−6,−6,−8,−8,−10]. These give the correct direction even when the ball is stuck against a wall (ball and trail in the same column).
- **u2–u9:** for each of the 4 direction hypotheses (down-right, down-left, up-right, up-left), one unit for "paddle < zone low end" and one for "paddle > zone high end". Each is 10·(table value for the ball cell) ∓ 10·(paddle column) − 5.

**Hidden layer 2 (8 units):** each unit is an AND of one comparator with the matching V and D.

**Output:** the right and left logits are +15 when one of their AND units is on and −15 otherwise. The other four actions are all no-ops and get logit 0.
